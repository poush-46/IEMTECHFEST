About TechNova

Where imagination meets implementation. For two decades, IEM has been the cradle of innovators.

Our Story

Born in 2005 as a small inter-department coding contest, TechNova has grown into Eastern India's most anticipated student tech festival. We blend cutting-edge technology, fierce competition, and vibrant culture into 72 unforgettable hours.

  • Organized by students, for students
  • 20,000+ alumni network
  • Industry mentors & recruiters on campus
